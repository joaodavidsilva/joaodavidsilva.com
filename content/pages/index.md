# Engineering *beyond* implementation.

I'm João David Silva, a Lead AI Engineer. When implementation gets cheap, the bottleneck moves to everything around the code: decisions, shared language and working with AI agents. This is where I write, research and build around that.

## How I work

Plan comprehensively. Build incrementally. *Evolve continuously.*

1. **Grill before building.** Before any code, the agent interviews me about the feature until there are no open questions left.
2. **Write the language down.** A shared glossary and an ADR for each significant decision. People and agents read the same words.
3. **Spec, then tickets.** Each ticket is small and verifiable on its own, without the rest of the plan in anyone's head.
4. **Implement, then look back.** A short retrospective feeds what we learned back into the plan, the glossary and the decisions.
