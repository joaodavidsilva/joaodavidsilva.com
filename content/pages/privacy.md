This notice covers joaodavidsilva.com. Projects with their own privacy policies (for example Facts Memorizer) say so on their Project page.

## Who is responsible

João David Silva. Write to [privacy@joaodavidsilva.com](mailto:privacy@joaodavidsilva.com) with any question or request about your data.

## No cookies

The site sets no cookies. If you choose a light or dark theme, your browser keeps that choice in its local storage, on your device only, so the next page opens in the theme you picked; nothing is sent to the site. Choosing System removes it. This is strictly necessary to provide the setting you asked for, so it needs no consent and there is no consent banner. The Turnstile bot check on the Lozza Project page is described under [Lozza Waitlist](#lozza-waitlist).

## Visit analytics

The site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free. It does not use cookies, local storage or fingerprinting to identify you, and it does not track you across sites. It records aggregate figures such as page views, referrers, countries, browsers and page load performance.

## Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. As the host, Cloudflare processes the technical data every web request carries, such as your IP address and browser user agent, to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Lozza Waitlist

The [Lozza Project page](/projects/lozza/) has a form for joining the Waitlist: the people who want to be told when Lozza becomes available. Joining is optional and needs your explicit consent, given by ticking the consent box.

- **What is stored:** your email address (trimmed and in lower case), the time you gave consent, the list you joined (Lozza) and the version of this notice in force at the time, identified by its last-updated date.
- **Why:** to tell you when Lozza is available, and to be able to show that you agreed to it. The address is used for nothing else and is never sold or shared.
- **Lawful basis:** your consent. You may withdraw it at any time by writing to [privacy@joaodavidsilva.com](mailto:privacy@joaodavidsilva.com); your sign-up is then deleted.
- **Where:** in a Cloudflare D1 database that only the site's own sign-up endpoint can reach. Nothing in the browser can read or change it.
- **How long:** until you leave the list, or until the list is no longer needed, at which point it is deleted.
- **Email:** nothing is sent yet. Before the first email goes out, this notice will name the service that sends it.

The form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which tells people from bots without a puzzle. To do so it processes signals such as your IP address, TLS fingerprint, browser user agent and the page you are on. Cloudflare describes these signals as strictly necessary for detecting bots, does not use them to identify, profile or target you, and also uses them to improve Turnstile. Turnstile runs only on the Lozza Project page. See the [Turnstile privacy addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/).

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data held about you, and you may complain to your data protection authority. Write to [privacy@joaodavidsilva.com](mailto:privacy@joaodavidsilva.com).
