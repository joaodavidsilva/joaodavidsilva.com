This notice covers joaodavidsilva.com. Projects with their own privacy policies (for example Facts Memorizer) say so on their Project page.

## Who is responsible

João David Silva. Write to [privacy@joaodavidsilva.com](mailto:privacy@joaodavidsilva.com) with any question or request about your data.

## No cookies

The site sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, so there is no consent banner.

## Visit analytics

The site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free. It does not use cookies, local storage or fingerprinting to identify you, and it does not track you across sites. It records aggregate figures such as page views, referrers, countries, browsers and page load performance.

## Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. As the host, Cloudflare processes the technical data every web request carries, such as your IP address and browser user agent, to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data held about you, and you may complain to your data protection authority. Write to [privacy@joaodavidsilva.com](mailto:privacy@joaodavidsilva.com).
